Radar

Find and identify nearby wireless devices, with pictures and a closer/farther finder.

Radar finds the wireless devices around you, works out what each one is (AirTags and other trackers, phones, AirPods and headphones, watches, cameras, TVs, computers, cars, smart-home gadgets) and groups them so you can tell them apart. A website cannot reach your phone's radios, so Radar is a small Python program you run in an app. Your device list never leaves your phone.

iPhone app: Bluetooth + Wi-Fi, pictures, finder

Get "Pythonista 3" from the App Store (paid, one time). Open it, swipe to the console (the >_ screen), paste this and tap Return:
import os,runpy,urllib.request as u;d=os.path.expanduser('~/Documents');o=u.build_opener();o.addheaders=[('User-Agent','Radar/1.0')];u.install_opener(o);[u.urlretrieve('https://radar.ackerworks.com/'+f,os.path.join(d,f)) for f in('radar_ios.py','wifi_scanner.py')];runpy.run_path(os.path.join(d,'radar_ios.py'),run_name='__main__')
Tap Allow for Bluetooth (and for local network when you tap Scan Wi-Fi). Tap any device for its picture, details and a live signal meter: walk around and watch the number rise as you get closer. Next time, open radar_ios.py in Pythonista and tap Run.
  • Sees everything advertising over Bluetooth Low Energy, on any network or none: most phones, AirPods and earbuds, AirTags, Tiles, SmartTags, watches, fitness bands, speakers, TVs, many cars, cameras and smart-home gadgets.
  • Scan Wi-Fi adds the devices on the network you are connected to.
  • iPhones do not let any app see Wi-Fi devices on other networks, Bluetooth Classic-only devices (older speakers, car stereos), or other radios such as Zigbee or cellular.

iPhone, free option (Wi-Fi network only, text list)

Install the free app "a-Shell" from the App Store, open it, and paste:
curl -O https://radar.ackerworks.com/wifi_scanner.py
python3 wifi_scanner.py
Tap Allow when iOS asks to find devices on your local network. The device list prints in a-Shell after about 40 seconds. Next time, just run the second line.

Android phone (everything on the phone)

Install Termux and Termux:API from F-Droid, then in Termux:
pkg install -y python termux-api curl
curl -O https://radar.ackerworks.com/wifi_scanner.py
python wifi_scanner.py

Mac or Linux computer

curl -O https://radar.ackerworks.com/wifi_scanner.py
python3 wifi_scanner.py

Windows computer

Needs Python from python.org. In PowerShell:
curl.exe -O https://radar.ackerworks.com/wifi_scanner.py
python wifi_scanner.py

Then

Open the link it prints. Use the "From phone" link to view it on any phone on the same Wi-Fi (this is how to use it with an iPhone).

Good to know

  • Only scan networks you own or are allowed to check.
  • The signal finder measures from the device running Radar, and can only range devices that broadcast their own Wi-Fi radio (routers, extenders, many printers, cameras and TVs).
  • Phones hide hardware addresses from apps, so scans run on a phone identify devices by name and services only. A computer scan also shows the hardware maker.
  • iPhones do not let apps read Wi-Fi signal strength, so on iPhone the finder works for Bluetooth devices only.
